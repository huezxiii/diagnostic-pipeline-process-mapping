Waiting time at each step

Mean minutes per stage. Only the analyzer run inside testing adds value.

How to read this: Each bar is one step of the journey. Longer bars mean samples waited there longer. Green is the only time a sample is being tested.

How long orders take

Share of orders by hours from order entry to verified result.

How to read this: Each bar shows the share of orders that got a result after that many hours. Bars bunched on the left mean fast, predictable results.

Sample arrivals vs intake capacity

Specimens reaching the central dock per hour on a typical day (3,288 orders/day).

How to read this: Bars show how many samples reach the lab each hour. The line shows how many the intake desk can log. Bars above the line build a queue.

STAT priority inversion

Average wait in the testing queue for each priority class, not counting analyzer time.

How to read this: Average minutes each type of order waits for testing. Real emergencies should wait the least.

Wasted minutes, biggest first

Minutes spent not adding value at each step, largest first. Hover for cumulative share.

How to read this: Steps ranked by minutes lost to waiting. Fixing the top few removes most of the delay.

Errors and repeat work

Share of orders affected by re-key errors, specimen rejections, or manual exceptions.

How to read this: Share of orders affected by re-key errors, specimen rejections, or manual exceptions. Lower is better.

Routes orders take

Orders grouped by the exception path they followed.

How to read this: Each row is a route an order took through the lab. Detours from the normal path add hours.
